Global average temperature anomaly (1880–2023)

Each bar shows how much warmer or cooler that year was compared to the 1951–1980 baseline. The warming trend is unmistakable since the 1980s.

Source: NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies (GISS)

Atmospheric CO₂ concentration (ppm)

CO₂ levels have risen 50% above pre-industrial levels and continue to climb each year due to burning fossil fuels and deforestation.

Source: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory / Mauna Loa Observatory

Global mean sea level rise (mm)

Thermal expansion of oceans and melting ice sheets are causing sea levels to rise at an accelerating rate, threatening coastal communities worldwide.

Source: NASA Satellite Sea Level Observations

Annual deforestation by region (million ha)

Tropical forests are being lost at alarming rates. South America and Africa are the most affected regions, with Southeast Asia close behind.

Source: Global Forest Watch / FAO

Extreme weather events per decade

The number of recorded extreme weather events has more than quintupled since the 1970s. Economic losses have increased even faster.

Source: World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
