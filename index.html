Key environmental issues
The data speaks clearly

Our GIS layers visualize four of the most pressing environmental challenges of our time.

+1.2°C
Rising Temperatures

Global average temperatures have risen 1.2°C above pre-industrial levels, with the last decade being the warmest on record. The Arctic is warming 4× faster than the rest of the planet.

23 cm
Sea Level Rise

Sea levels have risen 23 cm since 1880. Low-lying coastal nations and cities face growing flood risks. By 2100, sea levels may rise another 0.3–1 meter under current emissions trajectories.

−10M ha
Deforestation

An area the size of Portugal is lost to deforestation every year. The Amazon, Congo Basin, and Southeast Asian forests are disappearing at alarming rates, releasing stored carbon and destroying biodiversity.

×5
Extreme Weather

Extreme weather events have increased fivefold over the last 50 years. Heat waves, floods, droughts, and wildfires are becoming more frequent and intense as the climate warms.

Platform features
Built for exploration

ClimateMap combines real-world environmental datasets with powerful GIS tools to help you understand spatial patterns in climate data.

🗺️
Interactive GIS Map

Explore layered environmental data on an interactive map. Toggle between temperature anomalies, forest cover, sea-level projections, and more.

📊
Data Visualizations

Time-series charts and regional breakdowns help you see how climate indicators have changed over decades and how they vary across geography.

📍
Location Analysis

Click any location on the map to pull up climate statistics, historical trends, and risk scores for that specific region.

🛰️
Satellite Basemaps

Switch between street maps, topographic views, and satellite imagery to see land-use change and environmental features in their real-world context.

📏
Measurement Tools

Measure distances and areas affected by environmental events, from wildfire perimeters to flood inundation zones.

📤
Export & Share

Save map views and data snapshots to use in reports, presentations, or research papers. Support for common GIS data formats.
